# Project Scope & Scientific Boundaries
## RADSAFE — Patient-Centric Longitudinal CT Radiation Dose Tracking System

---

## 1. Core Objective
The objective of **RADSAFE** is to provide a unified, vendor-neutral platform where a patient's CT radiation dose history is consolidated under a **Unique Patient ID** across different scanners (Siemens, Philips, GE, Canon) and hospitals over multiple years.

---

## 2. In-Scope Features (What RADSAFE Will Do)
1. **Vendor-Neutral DICOM RDSR Parsing:** Read standard DICOM Radiation Dose Structured Reports generated by any CT manufacturer.
2. **Automated Dose Calculation:** Extract $\text{CTDI}_{\text{vol}}$ and $\text{DLP}$ and automatically compute Effective Dose ($\text{mSv}$) using published anatomical $k$-factors.
3. **Unique Patient Profile & Longitudinal Tracking:** Store patient records under a Unique Patient ID so that any future scan uploaded months or years later automatically appends to their cumulative exposure history.
4. **Multi-Year & Annual Dose Visualization:** Display charts illustrating cumulative radiation exposure over time, dose per anatomical region, and scanner distribution.
5. **Advisory DRL Comparison:** Compare individual examination doses against standard Diagnostic Reference Levels (DRLs) to highlight unusually high protocol exposures.
6. **PDF Patient Radiation Passport:** Export a clean, formatted PDF summary of the patient's entire CT radiation history for medical consultations.

---

## 3. Out-of-Scope Features (MVP Boundaries)
To ensure timely delivery and scientific accuracy, the following are explicitly excluded from the current scope:
1. **Direct PACS / Hospital HL7 Live Integration:** Files are ingested via manual web upload of `.dcm` RDSR files rather than live hospital network listeners.
2. **Optical Character Recognition (OCR) on Dose Screen Images:** RADSAFE processes structured DICOM RDSR files only (not scanned paper printouts or JPEG screenshots).
3. **Monte Carlo Organ Dose Simulation:** Effective Dose is estimated via standard DLP-to-$E$ conversion coefficients ($k$-factors), not patient-specific 3D voxel Monte Carlo simulations.
4. **Automated Clinical Decision Blocking:** RADSAFE does **not** forbid or block a physician from ordering a medically necessary CT scan.

---

## 4. Essential Medical Physics Disclaimers (Included in UI & PDF Reports)
* **No Regulatory Patient Dose Limit:** In medical radiation protection (ICRP Publication 103), **there is no statutory dose limit for patients** undergoing medically justified diagnostic procedures. Cumulative dose tracking serves as an informational decision-support tool to promote clinical justification and prevent redundant imaging.
* **Population-Based Estimate:** Effective Dose ($\text{mSv}$) calculated from $\text{DLP} \times k$ represents a standardized reference-phantom estimate for comparative tracking, not an exact measurement of individual biological risk.
* **Data Integrity Rule:** Missing DICOM attributes must always be reported as `Unavailable` and never coerced to `0`.
